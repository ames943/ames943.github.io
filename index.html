I'm a student at Santa Clara High School interested in computer science and economics. Most of my research starts with a public dataset and a claim people tend to take for granted, and then checks whether the data actually supports it. I write the analysis code for my own projects.

Does Default Stay Local? State Default and City Credit in the 1840s

When Pennsylvania stopped paying its debts in 1842, did investors also start charging Philadelphia and Pittsburgh more to borrow? Using weekly bond prices from the period, I find they didn't. The state's borrowing costs jumped while its cities' barely moved, which suggests investors judged each government on its own finances.

Cohort Effects, Not Treatment Response, Dominate Public Immunotherapy Cohorts: Evidence from Microbiome and Tumor Genomic Data

Researchers often combine public gut microbiome datasets to get more patients. Across four immunotherapy cohorts (283 patients), the differences between studies were far larger than any link to treatment response, and no correction method recovered a real signal. The paper comes with batch_detector, a tool that checks for this before pooling.
